GlidePad

Use your Android phone as a Bluetooth mouse, touchpad and keyboard for your computer. It pairs like any wireless mouse, so there is nothing to install on the computer. No internet permission. No ads. No data collected.

  • Touchpad: tap to click, two-finger scroll, hold to drag, right and middle click.
  • Air mouse: on phones with a gyroscope, hold the pad and point.
  • Keys: type with your phone's keyboard, with Esc, Tab, arrows and sticky Ctrl, Alt and Windows keys.
  • Media: play, pause and computer volume, also from your phone's volume buttons while GlidePad is open.
  • Works with Windows PCs, Macs and ChromeOS devices that accept Bluetooth mice and keyboards. Needs Android 9 or newer on a phone whose Bluetooth supports the HID Device profile.
