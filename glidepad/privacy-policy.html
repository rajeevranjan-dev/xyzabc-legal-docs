GlidePad Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

GlidePad is an Android app published on Google Play by XYZABC Technologies ("we", "our" or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information GlidePad handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short:
  • GlidePad does not collect, sell or share your personal data. We never receive any data from the app.
  • GlidePad has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no accounts.
  • What you type and how you move the pointer go directly to the computer you paired, over Bluetooth, and are not stored.

1. How GlidePad Works With Your Data

1.1 Input You Send to Your Computer

GlidePad makes your phone act as a Bluetooth mouse, keyboard and media remote (a Bluetooth "HID device"). While connected, it turns your touches, phone movement (in Air mouse), typed characters and button presses into standard mouse, keyboard and media-key signals and sends them in real time only to the computer you chose and paired with. Each connection happens because you start it. Bluetooth pairing encrypts the link between your phone and that computer. GlidePad does not record, log or keep this input, and it never reaches us. Once received, the input is handled by your computer like input from any other mouse or keyboard.

1.2 Typing

In Keys mode you type with the keyboard app installed on your phone (for example Gboard or Samsung Keyboard). GlidePad asks the keyboard not to show suggestions and not to learn from what you type. Your keyboard app may still have its own learning and privacy settings, which are covered by that keyboard's own privacy policy.

1.3 Motion Sensors

On phones with a gyroscope, Air mouse reads the gyroscope and gravity/accelerometer sensors only while you use Air mouse, to move the cursor. The readings are used immediately and are not stored.

1.4 Paired Computers

To show your computers, GlidePad reads the list of devices already paired with your phone from Android's Bluetooth settings. It saves the Bluetooth address of the last computer you connected to, so it can reconnect automatically. The notification shows the name of the computer you are connected to.

2. What Is Stored on Your Phone

GlidePad stores the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • your settings (pointer, air mouse and scroll speed, pointer acceleration, natural scrolling, tap to click, left-handed mode, right button, haptics, dim screen while connected, volume buttons control computer volume);
  • whether you have seen the gesture hints;
  • the Bluetooth address of the last computer you connected to.

This information is not sent to us. Android may include it in your own device backup (see below).

3. Android Backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include GlidePad's stored settings and the last computer's Bluetooth address in your device backup to your Google Account, and when you move to a new phone. These backups are created and managed by Google under your Google Account and Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot access them. On Android 9 and newer, Android encrypts backups with your screen lock when one is set. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

4. Permissions We Request

Nearby devices Bluetooth connect and advertise: to present your phone as a mouse and keyboard, make it visible for pairing when you ask, and connect to your computer. On Android 11 and older, the older Bluetooth permissions are used for the same purpose.
Notifications To show the ongoing "GlidePad is on" notification with its Turn off button.
Foreground service Connected device: to keep the Bluetooth connection to your computer alive while you switch apps or the screen is off.

GlidePad does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or phone permissions.

5. Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we never receive any. GlidePad contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

6. Google Play

When you download GlidePad, Google Play handles your download under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Google may give developers aggregated statistics and, if you allow it in your phone's settings, crash and performance reports. These come from Google, not from GlidePad.

7. Security

GlidePad keeps its data in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Input travels only over the encrypted Bluetooth link to the computer you paired. GlidePad does not run any server.

8. Keeping and Deleting Data

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete it, open Settings → Apps → GlidePad → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall GlidePad. To remove it from your device backup, manage or delete your backup in your Google Account or phone backup settings. To stop the phone from reconnecting to a computer, you can also remove the pairing in Bluetooth settings on the phone or the computer.

9. Children's Privacy

GlidePad is a general-purpose utility intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to This Policy

If GlidePad's handling of information changes, we will update this page and its "Last Updated" date before the change takes effect in the app.
